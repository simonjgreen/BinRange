# Screwless case for Makerfabs ESP32 UWB DW3000 (WROVER)

A two-part snap-together case for the Makerfabs ESP32 UWB DW3000 board, WROVER-E version (72 × 32.6 mm PCB). It needs no screws, nuts, glue or supports.

- The lid overlaps the base and is held by four shallow snap beads. Squeeze the long sides near the catches to open it.
- Four tapered pins locate the board through its mounting holes, and four sprung pads in the lid hold it down without rattling.
- The USB port is open at one end, and there are access holes for the reset and flash buttons.
- The lid has a centred vent pattern.
- Closed size: 86 × 46.6 × 17.6 mm.

Also available as a DIN rail version, which uses the same lid with a snap-on TS35 clip.

## Files

- `wrover-case-standalone-v7.1.0.3mf`: base and lid on one plate, in print orientation
- `wrover-case-v7.1.0-base.stl`, `wrover-case-v7.1.0-lid.stl`: the same parts as individual STLs

## Print settings

- PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16 mm layers
- 4 walls, 5 top and 5 bottom layers, 20% infill
- No supports. Print both parts outside face down, as laid out. Don't scale them.
- Keep automatic supports out of the spring slots and pin sockets in the lid.

## Assembly

1. Lower the board onto the four pins with the USB connector at the open end.
2. Line the lid's button holes up with the two buttons.
3. Press the lid straight down, near each catch in turn, until all four click.
4. To open, squeeze the long base walls just below the lid near the catches and lift that end.

The board should rest on all four supports before you close the lid. If the pins are oversized on your printer, don't force it.

## Status

The previous revision was printed and tested with the board fitted. This version adds wider locating pins and centred vents, and it is the maker's current working design. No force or endurance measurements have been made.
